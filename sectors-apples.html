Understanding fruit condition

Bitter pit needs a wider look.

A fruit Calcium result is one part of the investigation. The playbook brings variety and rootstock, crop load, water movement, harvest maturity and storage into the same review.

That wider picture helps the team decide what needs checking before changing a product or rate. Existing test results and what you have seen in the block are useful places to start.

Across the season

Each season informs the next.

The booklet describes six broad periods of development. Actual growth stage and block conditions guide the timing; a calendar month alone does not.

01

Post-harvest

Review the crop removed, pack-out and remaining canopy when considering the post-harvest prescription.

02

Senescence and dormancy

Bring soil results and the season’s history into planning as the trees move towards dormancy.

03

Bud development and flowering

Assess spring development against stored reserves, conditions and the crop the block is expected to carry.

04

Fruit set and cell division

Revisit crop load and early fruit development alongside the available plant results.

05

Fruit expansion and fill

Consider ongoing fruit growth with canopy condition, water supply and the block’s nutrition results.

06

Maturation and pre-harvest

Assess colour and maturity with fruit condition and the intended storage and market requirements.

The Hybrid-Ag approach

Position. Pattern. Plan.

The playbook uses these questions to connect the evidence with a practical recommendation. Custom prescription blends begin with the requirements of the block.

01Position

Where is the block now?

Variety, rootstock, growth stage, crop load and the test results available today.

02Pattern

What has changed?

Previous results and applications, seasonal conditions, and what harvest and storage showed.

03Plan

What needs to happen next?

A prescription suited to the block, the application equipment and your budget, with any further testing agreed with the team.

Water

Plant results interpreted against sampling context and actual crop development.

Fruit quality, crop removals and pack-out used to inform the next review.
