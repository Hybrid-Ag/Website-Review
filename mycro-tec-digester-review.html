Before use

Applying Digester after harvest.

Digester is sprayed onto crop residue after harvest. Apply as close to rain as practical, when there is moisture in the residue and soil, and avoid the heat of the day.

The rates below follow the manufacturer’s technical sheet. This page and brochure are review drafts; confirm the current product label and Safety Data Sheet with the team before use.

Rates and application
Manufacturer’s technical-sheet rates · per hectare
ApplicationRate
Standard crop residue1–1.5 L/ha Digester
Extra-heavy residueUp to 3 L/ha Digester
Ammonium Sulphate starter8 kg/ha
Water60–100 L/ha

Keep the Ammonium Sulphate at 8 kg/ha, even when the Digester rate is increased for heavy residue. Do not increase the starter with the residue load.

Apply once after harvest using a boomspray or orchard sprayer directed onto the residue. Digester is not for in-crop application and is not recommended for nut trees.

Preparation and mixing

Shake the container well before measuring. Use cool, clean, non-chlorinated water. A neutral pH is preferred; do not use water below pH 5.5.

Add Digester to the water first, then add the specified Ammonium Sulphate starter and stir thoroughly. Do not substitute the starter or add other products to this mix.

Strain as you pour and use nozzles and filters of 150 µm or larger. Mix only what can be applied that day. Do not leave the diluted mix in the tank overnight; fungal growth can block nozzles.

Storage and handling

Store in the original labelled container, upright in a clean, dry place out of direct sunlight, between 5°C and 40°C. Protect it from sudden temperature changes and do not freeze.

The stated shelf life is 12 months from manufacture when stored as directed. Check the expiry date, use opened product promptly and never store the diluted mix. Follow the closure instructions for the container supplied.

Wear gloves, long sleeves and eye protection when mixing. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid breathing spray mist. Wash hands after handling and before eating. Keep out of reach of children and follow the current label and Safety Data Sheet.
