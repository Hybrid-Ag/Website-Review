Crop nutrition

Review the Iron requirement and soil conditions.

IronMan supplies Iron as HBED and EDDHA chelates for a correction through the soil. The chelated formulation is considered where Iron availability around the roots needs attention, rather than as a general liquid feed for several Nutrients.

The soil conditions and crop-test information help the team assess the requirement and intended placement. IronMan is supplied by weight, so its preparation and rate basis must remain separate from litre-pack products. A soil Iron correction also needs to be considered alongside the rest of the crop’s nutrition, rather than selected from leaf colour alone.

This product may be suitable for what your crop needs now. As always, test, don’t guess. Your results help us work out whether it is appropriate, or whether a liquid, dry or granular prescription blend would better address specific Nutrient shortages or imbalances.
