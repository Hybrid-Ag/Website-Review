Production priorities

What does this crop need to achieve?

The variety and intended market affect the grades you are aiming for. Grading and storage results matter as well as the crop picked.

01

Fruit size and yield

Yield and the spread of fruit sizes help show how much of the crop can meet the intended grade.

02

Colour and maturity

Colour alone does not show whether fruit is ready to pick. Other maturity measures need to be checked too.

03

Fruit condition

Firmness and fruit finish matter at picking and after storage. Problems such as bitter pit need a review of the whole season.

04

Storage and pack-out

Grading and storage results show how much fruit meets specification after harvest. They help assess the block’s performance beyond fruit size at picking.

Understanding the block

Read the results with the block’s history.

The team reviews test results with the block’s conditions and history before planning the prescription. The recommendation also needs to suit your application equipment and budget.

01

Market and storage target

Fruit size, maturity and condition need to suit the market and intended storage period.

02

Variety and crop

We need the variety and rootstock, actual growth stage, crop load and an assessment of tree growth to interpret the results.

03

Soil and water

Soil and water results need to be checked with the root-zone conditions in the orchard. The water used for irrigation and applications also affects the recommendation.

04

Season and history

Previous tests, applications and growing conditions help explain changes through the season. Harvest maturity, storage performance and pack-out help the team investigate fruit condition and plan the next prescription.

This sample explains the approach; it is not a prescription for your block. The team confirms products, rates and timing after reviewing your results and block history.
