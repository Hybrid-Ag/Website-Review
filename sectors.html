Horticulture

Nutrition for tree and berry crops through budburst, flowering, fruit development, harvest and post-harvest.

Production priorities
Marketable yield, pack-out and grade, fruit quality, and condition through storage or shelf life.
Crops covered
Dedicated Apple and Cherry guides are available, with sample nutrition programs to review with the team.

02Vegetable crops

Small Crop

Review short-cycle crops during growth and use the harvest results when planning the next planting.

Production priorities
Marketable yield, customer specification, uniformity and timing, and crop-specific quality or holding measures.
Crops covered
Leafy greens and herbs, brassicas, roots and alliums, potatoes and other tuber crops.

03Cropping and pasture

Broadacre & Pasture

Recommendations account for seasonal conditions, rotation history, paddock zones and representative testing.

Production priorities
Establishment and uniformity, yield and crop quality, input efficiency, and usable pasture growth and persistence.
Crops covered
Cereals, oilseeds, pulses, lucerne and mixed pasture systems.
Explore Broadacre & Pasture Request Broadacre testing
04Vineyards

Viticulture

Vineyard advice takes account of variety and rootstock, region, crop load and seasonal conditions, as well as winery or market requirements.

Production priorities
Crop and canopy, yield and specification, fruit maturity and composition, condition and harvest timing.
Crops covered
Wine and table grapes, with recommendations suited to the intended destination for the fruit.

The Hybrid-Ag approach

Position. Pattern. Plan.

These questions help the team understand the growing system and make recommendations from the available information.

01Position

Where are you now?

Start with the crop, its growth stage and location, current conditions and any test results you have.

02Pattern

How did you get here?

Compare previous results and applications with changes in the crop and growing conditions.

03Plan

What should happen next?

Agree on the next practical step and how its effects will be assessed.
