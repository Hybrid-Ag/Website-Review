Product use

Choose the treatment for the intended crop response.

Eco-Gibb supplies gibberellic acid as a soluble granule. It is a plant growth regulator, so the decision concerns a particular developmental treatment rather than the amount of Nitrogen or another Nutrient to supply.

The product material discusses different purposes and timings, including fruit set, berry development and maturity management. These are not interchangeable treatments or a recommendation for every crop. The current label and agronomist need to establish the authorised crop, intended purpose and timing before the product is prepared.

Testing and a discussion about your crop and soil help us decide what is needed. A liquid, dry or granular prescription blend can address the Nutrient requirement; the team can also advise whether this product has a separate, useful role.
