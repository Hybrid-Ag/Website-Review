Crop nutrition

Account for the fish product’s Nutrient contribution.

Bio-Sea Fish supplies Nitrogen and amino acids from fish-derived material in a liquid fertiliser. It gives the team a fish-based option to assess during active crop growth, with its declared Nutrient contribution included alongside soil supply and other applications.

Account for its Nitrogen when reviewing other feeds and the amount still required. Soil and plant information help the team decide whether a fish-based product is appropriate, or whether a different Nutrient balance is needed. The amino-acid formulation is part of that discussion, not a reason to overlook a shortage of another Element.

This product may be suitable for what your crop needs now. As always, test, don’t guess. Your results help us work out whether it is appropriate, or whether a liquid, dry or granular prescription blend would better address specific Nutrient shortages or imbalances.
