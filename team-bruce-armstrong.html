About Bruce

Experience in soil and plant nutrition.

Bruce starts by listening to the grower and observing the paddock. He uses test results to help assess what needs attention.

His experience includes irrigated crops and pasture, as well as broadacre farming systems.

Bruce recalls helping a grower better understand soil health, particularly the role of Calcium. Explaining the reasoning behind his advice is an important part of his approach.

Soil and plant nutrition advice for broadacre growers across Southern NSW.

Beyond the field

Family and farming.

Outside his agronomy work, Bruce spends time with his family and works on the land. Family, faith and farming are important to him, and he regards primary production as both a responsibility and a privilege.

Bruce also shares his experience beyond paddock visits. He joined James White for a Hybrid-Ag webinar on Nitrogen use efficiency, covering foliar nutrition, sap testing and soil biology. A link to the recording is available on this page.
