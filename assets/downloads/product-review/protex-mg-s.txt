Overview
Protex Mg S supplies Magnesium and Sulphur in a liquid foliar pasture input. Magnesium is part of chlorophyll, while Sulphur is needed in plant proteins.
The pasture requirement needs to be assessed alongside soil supply and Nitrogen nutrition. Plant results and the growing conditions help the team decide whether this combination is useful during active growth.
