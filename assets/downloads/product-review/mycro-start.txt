Overview
Mycro-Start is a liquid extract of worm-digested compost. It brings a naturally mixed microbial population to the root zone, rather than the fixed composition of a mineral fertiliser or a single selected organism.
The population varies between batches, so the actual product supplied, preparation and storage matter. Review the intended placement with the team alongside mineral nutrition. Seed treatments, root dips and soil applications need their own preparation and dose; directions for one method should not be transferred to another.
