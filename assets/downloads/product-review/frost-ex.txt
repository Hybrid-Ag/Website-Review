Overview
Frost-Ex combines Potassium and Phosphorus with kelp and carbohydrates. The material describes applications ahead of frost-risk periods, including green tip in perennial crops when young growth or reproductive tissue can be exposed to cold conditions.
The formulation still needs to be assessed as part of the crop’s nutrition. Its Potassium and Phosphorus contributions belong in the recommendation, with the proposed timing considered against crop conditions and current directions. Product use is not a guarantee of protection from frost damage or a replacement for the farm’s other frost-management decisions.
