Overview
ToNik supplies Nitrogen in a liquid formulation with fulvic and carbohydrate components. It provides foliar and watered-in options for Broadacre crops and pasture when the crop needs Nitrogen.
Winter growth is affected by more than nutrition alone. Soil Nitrogen, moisture, temperature and grazing pressure help the team assess a pasture application; the same attention to crop demand and previous inputs guides an in-season Broadacre recommendation.
