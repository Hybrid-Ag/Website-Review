Overview
ToNik supplies Nitrogen in a liquid formulation with fulvic and carbohydrate components. It provides foliar and watered-in options for Broadacre crops and pasture when the crop needs Nitrogen.
Winter growth is affected by more than nutrition alone. We consider soil Nitrogen and moisture, along with temperature and grazing pressure, when assessing pasture. For an in-season Broadacre application, we also need to know crop demand and what has already been supplied.
