Mycro-Tec VAM | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

A root-zone mycorrhizal inoculant.
Mycro-Tec VAM is a mycorrhizal fungal inoculant supplied as a powder for the root zone.
It is considered within the biological part of the soil recommendation, alongside the crop’s mineral requirements.

Overview
Mycro-Tec VAM supplies mycorrhizal fungi for the root zone. These fungi form an association with plant roots, with fungal threads extending into the surrounding soil beyond the root surface. That association is relevant to the discussion about access to Phosphorus and other soil Nutrients.
The powder’s preparation and placement need to suit the crop and establishment conditions. It is considered alongside the mineral prescription, rather than counted as a set amount of Phosphorus supplied by a fertiliser. Discuss the crop and root-zone conditions with the team and confirm the current specification and preparation directions before use.
