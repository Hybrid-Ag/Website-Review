Amplify | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Spray-water conditioning and wetting.
Amplify is a multi-functional spray adjuvant designed to reduce the pH of spray water.
It is used with foliar nutrition applications. It is not a fertiliser or a substitute for correcting a Nutrient shortage.

Overview
The staged material describes Amplify for spray-water conditioning and the way droplets contact the leaf. Water quality and the products in the proposed mixture need to be reviewed together.
Water quality can affect the choice of an adjuvant, but it does not tell us which Nutrients the crop needs. We can help you assess the water and proposed mixture, together with the equipment and foliar application being considered.
