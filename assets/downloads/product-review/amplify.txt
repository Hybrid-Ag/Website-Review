Amplify | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Spray-water conditioning and wetting.
Amplify is a spray adjuvant combining water-conditioning, wetting and sticking functions.
It is used with foliar nutrition applications. It is not a fertiliser or a substitute for correcting a Nutrient shortage.

Overview
The staged material describes Amplify for spray-water conditioning and the way droplets contact the leaf. Water quality and the products in the proposed mixture need to be reviewed together.
The reason for adding an adjuvant is different from choosing the Nutrients for the crop. The team can assess whether this product is appropriate for the water, equipment and foliar application being considered.

Application
Follow the current Amplify directions and those of the other products in the mixture. Confirm mixing order and compatibility for the proposed application; do not assume every nutrition mixture requires an adjuvant.
