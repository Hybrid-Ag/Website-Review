Overview
Humix supplies Potassium humate derived from plant material in a liquid form. Humic compounds are the main part of its formulation, with a smaller Potassium contribution to include in the root-zone nutrition calculation.
The material describes soil application and fertigation alongside the Nutrients selected for the crop. Review the planned feed and the soil conditions with the team before adding Humix. Its humic character does not replace the need to establish the mineral requirement, or prove compatibility with every irrigation mixture.
