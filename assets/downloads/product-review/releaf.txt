Overview
ReLeaf combines Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium with amino acids in a liquid foliar fertiliser. It offers a combined in-season option for Broadacre crops and pasture where more than one of those Nutrients is needed.
Plant testing helps us establish what the crop needs before recommending another application. We compare ReLeaf’s fixed contribution with crop demand and previous inputs. Where a different balance is required, we can recommend a custom liquid prescription.
