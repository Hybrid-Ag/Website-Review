Overview
BudMax Organic combines Manganese, Zinc, Boron, Molybdenum and Iron in one liquid product. It offers a mixed trace option where more than one of these Elements is needed as the crop develops.
Compare the fixed combination with the crop’s plant results and the Nutrients already supplied. The trace requirement can be reviewed during growth or after harvest, with timing checked against this Organic formulation’s current directions. Each Element needs to be accounted for rather than assuming every crop needs the same trace application.
