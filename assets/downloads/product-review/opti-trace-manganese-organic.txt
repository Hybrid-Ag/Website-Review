Overview
Opti-Trace Manganese Organic supplies Manganese and Sulphur in a liquid formulation with amino acid, fulvic and saponin carriers. Manganese has a role in photosynthesis, making its supply relevant throughout crop growth.
The crop results determine whether an application is needed. A targeted correction can be compared with the Manganese already supplied, without assuming flowering or fruit set alone establishes a shortage.
