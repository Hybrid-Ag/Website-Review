Overview
Pearl Boost Organic supplies Carbon and plant Nutrients in a low-solubility granule. It is a soil application, including banded use in tree crops, rather than a foliar correction.
The soil and crop requirement determine whether this product’s contribution is appropriate. Placement and soil moisture are part of that assessment, alongside the Nutrients already supplied and any custom dry prescription.
