Liquid Magnesium with Nitrogen.
Power-Mag supplies Magnesium with Nitrogen and Boron in a liquid formulation using sugar-alcohol complexes.
It is considered where the crop requires a Magnesium correction during active growth.

Overview
Magnesium is part of chlorophyll. Power-Mag supplies it with Nitrogen and Boron in a liquid using sugar alcohol complexes, for a crop that needs a Magnesium correction during active growth.
The formulation includes spreading and penetrant agents for leaf application. The agronomist can compare the full Nutrient contribution with plant results and previous applications before deciding whether Power-Mag matches the requirement.
