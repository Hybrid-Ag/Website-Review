Overview
Opti-Trace Cobalt supplies Cobalt with Sulphur in a liquid trace fertiliser. The product is considered in legume nutrition where the crop and soil assessment identify a Cobalt requirement.
Cobalt is relevant to the bacteria associated with Nitrogen fixation in legume roots. A Cobalt recommendation is therefore different from simply adding more Nitrogen, and needs to be assessed against the crop’s actual conditions.
