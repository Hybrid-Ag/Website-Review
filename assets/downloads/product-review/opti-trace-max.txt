Overview
Opti-Trace MAX combines Manganese, Zinc, Copper, Boron, Iron and Molybdenum in a chelated liquid. The formulation includes heptonate, sugar alcohols, amino acids, fulvic extracts and saponins. It offers a broader trace option where crop testing identifies several of these Elements as needing attention.
High-pH and sandy soils can give the agronomist useful context, but soil type alone does not establish every trace shortage. The crop results help decide whether MAX matches the need or whether a custom prescription would provide a better balance.
