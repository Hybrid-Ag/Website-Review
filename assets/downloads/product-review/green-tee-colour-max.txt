Iron, Magnesium and Manganese for turf.
Green-Tee Colour Max combines Iron, Magnesium and Manganese in a liquid turf formulation.
The staged description positions it where those Nutrients are required without a substantial additional Nitrogen contribution.

Overview
Green-Tee Colour Max supplies Iron, Magnesium and Manganese with very little Nitrogen. It is a different option from a Nitrogen-led turf feed when those Nutrients need attention and another substantial Nitrogen addition is not required.
The material describes foliar and watered-in use on warm- and cool-season turf. Review the canopy condition with test information and the existing feed before choosing the formulation. Appearance alone cannot establish the cause of poor colour, or show that all the Nutrients in a fixed blend are needed.
