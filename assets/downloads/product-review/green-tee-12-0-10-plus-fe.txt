Overview
Green-Tee 12.0.10 + Fe combines a Nitrogen and Potassium feed with Iron in the same liquid application. It gives turf managers a fixed-analysis option when the recommendation calls for growth nutrition and an Iron contribution together.
An Iron requirement should not obscure the other Nutrients supplied. Compare the complete grade with the existing turf feed, test results and growing conditions before selecting it. That helps determine whether this combination meets the requirement or whether Iron should be addressed without the same Nitrogen and Potassium addition.
