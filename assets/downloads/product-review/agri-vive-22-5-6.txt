Overview
Agri-Vive 22.5.6 is a Nitrogen-led liquid grade with smaller Phosphorus and Potassium contributions. It is considered when Nitrogen is the main requirement and the other Nutrients supplied are also needed. This is different from choosing a grade with similar amounts of each major Nutrient.
The planned application needs to account for more than its Nitrogen content. Compare the full analysis with the nutrition already available and the demand of the growing crop. That helps the team decide whether this fixed combination is appropriate or whether a prescription should supply different proportions.
