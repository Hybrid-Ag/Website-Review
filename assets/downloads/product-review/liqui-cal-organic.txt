Overview
Liqui-Cal Organic supplies Calcium in a micronised liquid suspension for the root zone. It gives the team a liquid-delivery option to consider during the growing season when Calcium is required, using this formulation’s own analysis and directions.
The preparation and irrigation method matter as well as the amount of Calcium supplied. We review the equipment and soil conditions with you, allowing for the nutrition already applied. Current certification and permitted methods need to be checked for Liqui-Cal Organic; the conventional product’s documents cannot establish those details.
