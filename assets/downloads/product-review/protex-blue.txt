Overview
Protex Blue combines Boron, Copper and Molybdenum in a liquid trace blend with heptonate, sugar alcohol and fulvic components. It is a foliar option for Broadacre crops when testing identifies a need for that combination.
The recommendation starts with the crop results, not the product alone. The agronomist can compare each Element’s contribution with previous applications before choosing Protex Blue or adjusting a custom liquid prescription.
