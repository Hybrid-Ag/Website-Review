Overview
Power-Cal supplies Calcium with Nitrogen and Boron in a liquid using sugar alcohol complexes. It is considered while new roots, shoots and fruit tissue develop and the crop needs a Calcium contribution.
Calcium is needed in developing tissue, but supply cannot be assessed separately from irrigation and root condition. Plant results and the crop stage help the agronomist decide whether Power-Cal is useful or whether the prescription should provide a different Nutrient balance.
