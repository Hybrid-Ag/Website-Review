REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Liquid Calcium and Phosphorus suspension.
Liqui-Phos Organic is a micronised guano suspension supplying Calcium and Phosphorus.
It is considered in the root-zone nutrition discussion, with its own directions and certification checked before selection.

Overview
Liqui-Phos Organic supplies Calcium and Phosphorus together in finely ground guano, prepared as a liquid suspension. It offers a root-zone option to assess where the crop requires both Nutrients rather than a Phosphorus correction alone.
The full contribution needs to be reviewed against the soil results and previous inputs. Preparation of the suspension and the intended delivery equipment also matter. Confirm the current Organic-specific directions and certification before application; a similarly named conventional product cannot establish those requirements.
