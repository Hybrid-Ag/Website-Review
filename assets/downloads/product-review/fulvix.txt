REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Liquid fulvic compounds.
Fulvix is a liquid fulvic-acid product used alongside crop nutrition.
Its purpose and the proposed mixture need to be assessed separately from the amount of each Nutrient the crop requires.

Overview
Fulvix provides fulvic compounds in a liquid form to consider alongside a Nutrient application. These compounds can bind minerals, which is why the product is discussed separately from a fertiliser chosen to supply a fixed amount of Nitrogen, Phosphorus or another Element.
The planned Nutrients, mixture and placement help determine whether Fulvix is appropriate. It cannot replace the calculation of what the crop needs, and the general properties of fulvic compounds do not prove that every mixture is compatible. Review the actual products and current directions with the team before combining them.
