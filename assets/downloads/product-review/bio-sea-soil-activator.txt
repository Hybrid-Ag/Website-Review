Overview
Bio-Sea Soil Activator combines fish-derived material with fulvic components for soil application ahead of cropping. The fish-based material is included as a food source for soil microbes, alongside the product’s Nutrient contribution around developing roots. It is assessed within the soil recommendation rather than as a general foliar feed.
Placement matters because the intended use is around the future root zone. Review the soil results, the crop being established and the available delivery equipment with the team. The organic material and mineral contribution should be considered together with any liquid, dry or granular prescription required for the paddock or block.
