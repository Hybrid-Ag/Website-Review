Overview
Opti-Trace Cobalt Organic supplies Cobalt with Sulphur in a liquid product. It is considered where a legume crop needs Cobalt as part of its trace Nutrient recommendation.
The recommendation needs to account for the soil supply and the crop’s conditions. It should be a targeted correction, not a routine addition to every legume application.
