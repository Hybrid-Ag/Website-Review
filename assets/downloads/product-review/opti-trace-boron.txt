Opti-Trace Boron | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Targeted liquid Boron nutrition.
Opti-Trace Boron supplies Boron in a liquid formulation.
It is considered where the crop requires a Boron correction, with the amount set against test information and the other Boron already supplied.

Overview
Boron is needed as new tissues develop and has a role in flowering and early fruit development. Opti-Trace Boron supplies this Element in a targeted liquid product, rather than adding a wider trace blend.
The crop’s Boron requirement and the amount already supplied need to be assessed together. Soil and plant results help the agronomist decide whether a separate Boron application is useful or whether the liquid prescription should be adjusted.
