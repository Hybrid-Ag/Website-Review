Overview
Green-Tee 10.0.10 supplies Nitrogen and Potassium in similar proportions for turf. It provides a liquid grade to assess where both Nutrients are needed, without selecting a more Phosphorus-led feed.
The existing turf feed and test information help determine whether that balance is appropriate. Count both contributions against the requirement and the nutrition already supplied. If the turf needs a different proportion or an additional Element, the team can assess a prescription instead of increasing the whole fixed grade to meet one shortage.
