Mycro-Tec SP | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

A liquid root-zone microbial inoculant.
Mycro-Tec SP is described as a liquid microbial inoculant for the soil around plant roots.
It is considered within the soil-biology recommendation alongside mineral supply, not as a fixed-analysis Nutrient blend.

Overview
The documented SP material describes a blend of microorganisms for application around plant roots. It is considered within the biological part of the soil recommendation as the crop establishes, alongside the Nutrients available in the root zone.
Confirm the formulation being supplied and its preparation and water requirements with the team. An inoculant and a mineral fertiliser make different contributions to the recommendation, so the intended placement and soil conditions need to be considered with the crop’s wider nutrition needs.

Composition source: dashboard staging. Basis: CFU/mL

Application
Ask for the directions approved for the SP stock being supplied. Confirm preparation, water requirements, placement and storage before applying the inoculant.
