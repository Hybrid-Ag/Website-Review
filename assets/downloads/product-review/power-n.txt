Overview
Power-N supplies Nitrogen in a liquid formulation with sugar and fulvic components. Its foliar, liquid-injection and fertigation uses provide different ways to make an application when the crop needs Nitrogen.
The amount needed depends on crop demand, soil supply and previous applications. The team can compare Power-N with those requirements and explain whether a standard Nitrogen product or a custom liquid prescription would be more appropriate.
