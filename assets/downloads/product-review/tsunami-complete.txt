Overview
Tsunami Complete combines block co-polymer surfactants with humic acid in a turf wetting formulation. It is considered for water-repellent soil and localised dry spots, particularly in sandy root zones.
The soil profile and irrigation pattern help establish where a wetting treatment is needed. This is a water-management decision rather than an NPK feed, and should be assessed separately from the turf’s Nutrient prescription.
