Overview
Opti-Cal Organic supplies Calcium and Boron in liquid form. It is considered where the crop needs those Nutrients as new tissue and fruit develop.
Compare the product’s contribution with the block’s test information and previous applications. Water supply and root condition also belong in a Calcium assessment, so the application needs to be considered with the way the block is managed.
