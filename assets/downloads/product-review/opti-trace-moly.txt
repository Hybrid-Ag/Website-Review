REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Targeted liquid Molybdenum.
Opti-Trace Moly supplies Molybdenum in a chelated liquid formulation.
It is considered for a confirmed trace requirement alongside the crop’s Nitrogen nutrition, not as a general replacement for Nitrogen supply.

Overview
Molybdenum is involved in enzymes used in nitrate metabolism and in Nitrogen fixation by legume root bacteria. Opti-Trace Moly provides a targeted liquid source when the crop needs this trace Element.
Adding Nitrogen does not correct a shortage of Molybdenum. The agronomist can assess the crop’s Molybdenum status alongside the Nitrogen supply before deciding whether a separate correction belongs in the recommendation.
