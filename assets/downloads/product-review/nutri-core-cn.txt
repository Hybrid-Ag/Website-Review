REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Combined liquid Calcium and Nitrogen.
Nutri-Core CN supplies Calcium and Nitrogen in a liquid fertiliser.
It is considered where the crop requires both Nutrients during growth, with the complete analysis assessed alongside other applications.

Overview
Calcium is an essential Nutrient for development of all plant tissue.
When Calcium is needed, we also account for the Nitrogen supplied with it. The soil and plant results help us work out what is required at that stage of growth, allowing for previous applications. We can then discuss whether CN is suitable or the prescription needs a different balance.
