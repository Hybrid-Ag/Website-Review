Liquid Magnesium and Sulphur.
Opti-Mag supplies Magnesium and Sulphur in a liquid fertiliser.
The staged material describes foliar and fertigation use where the crop’s Magnesium requirement needs attention during growth.

Overview
Magnesium is part of the chlorophyll molecule, so it is needed as the crop builds and maintains its canopy. Opti-Mag supplies Magnesium with Sulphur in a liquid formulation for a crop that needs these Nutrients.
The product has foliar and irrigation uses. Crop testing helps establish whether Magnesium supply needs attention, while the complete analysis and previous applications help the agronomist choose the correction.
