Overview
Cell-Ex combines Phosphorus and Calcium with Nitrogen, Potassium and trace Nutrients in a liquid foliar feed. The material describes use during fruit development and grain or seed filling, when the crop requires the Nutrients carried in the product.
Developing crops do not all need the same proportions of Nutrients. We compare the full analysis with the crop’s test results and crop load, allowing for the nutrition already supplied. That helps us decide whether a combined foliar feed is appropriate or a prescription should address a more specific shortage.
