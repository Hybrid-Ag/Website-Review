Overview
Cell-Ex combines Phosphorus and Calcium with Nitrogen, Potassium and trace Nutrients in a liquid foliar feed. The material describes use during fruit development and grain or seed filling, when the crop requires the Nutrients carried in the product.
A developing crop does not automatically need every Nutrient in the same proportions. The full analysis lets the team compare this fixed combination with the test results, crop load and nutrition already supplied. That comparison is important before choosing a combined foliar feed instead of a prescription aimed at a more specific shortage.
