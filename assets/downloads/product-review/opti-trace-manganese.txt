REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Liquid Manganese trace nutrition.
Opti-Trace Manganese supplies Manganese with Sulphur in a chelated liquid formulation.
It provides a targeted option where the crop needs Manganese, rather than automatically adding a broader trace blend.

Overview
Manganese is involved in photosynthesis. Opti-Trace Manganese supplies this Element with Sulphur in a chelated liquid for a crop whose Manganese requirement is not being met.
The liquid has foliar and watered-in uses. Plant results help establish the requirement, and the agronomist can account for Manganese already supplied before choosing a targeted application or a mixed liquid prescription.
