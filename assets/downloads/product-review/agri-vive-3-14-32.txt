Overview
Agri-Vive 3.14.32 provides Potassium and Phosphorus together with a smaller Nitrogen component. It gives the team a liquid grade to assess where the first two Nutrients are required but a larger Nitrogen addition would not match the crop. The smaller Nitrogen contribution still needs to be included.
Review the complete grade against soil supply and the plant-test results, not just the largest figure in its name. Previous applications also affect the amount still needed. This comparison helps distinguish a suitable fixed-analysis feed from a requirement for a prescription with different proportions.
