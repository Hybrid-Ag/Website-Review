Overview
Opti-Trace Bio-Check Organic supplies Magnesium, Sulphur and Molybdenum with other trace Elements. Its liquid formulation includes amino acid chelates, fulvic extracts and saponins.
The blend is considered when the crop needs several of the supplied Nutrients together. Plant testing and previous applications help establish that need and avoid adding a mixed trace product where a more targeted prescription would be appropriate.
