Overview
Green-Tee Conditioner combines Calcium, Nitrogen and Boron with carbohydrate and fulvic components in a liquid turf feed. It is an option where the turf needs those Nutrients together.
The Calcium, Nitrogen and Boron each need to be included in the recommendation. We look at previous applications and the soil and plant results to establish what the turf still needs. The name Conditioner does not tell us whether these Nutrients are short or whether the proportions are suitable.
