Overview
Green-Tee Conditioner combines Calcium, Nitrogen and Boron with carbohydrate and fulvic components in a liquid turf feed. The formulation gives the team a combined option to assess where those Nutrients are required together.
Calcium, Nitrogen and Boron each need to be accounted for when reviewing the planned application. The turf’s previous inputs and soil and plant information help determine whether the proportions are suitable. The product name alone cannot establish what conditioning is needed or justify adding Nutrients already present in sufficient amounts.
