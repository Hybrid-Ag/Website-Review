Liquid amino-acid Nitrogen.
Amino-Plex Liquid supplies Nitrogen in an amino-acid formulation derived from plant and marine materials.
This page is for the liquid product. The separate powder has its own composition, supply and application requirements.

Overview
Amino-Plex Liquid supplies Nitrogen in an amino-acid formulation derived from plant and marine materials. Its liquid format allows applications to be split through the growing season where the crop needs Nitrogen, rather than treating every growth stage as a reason for the same application.
The liquid product information describes foliar, liquid-injection and streaming methods. We need to know which method you intend to use and how much Nitrogen is already available to the crop. The liquid and powder are separate products, with different preparation and supply requirements; the directions must relate to the product being used.
