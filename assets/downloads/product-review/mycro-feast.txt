It is described for root-zone use within the soil recommendation. It is different from a microbial inoculant that supplies selected organisms.

Overview
Mycro-Feast combines food sources for soil fungi and bacteria, including fish, kelp and humic and fulvic materials. Its purpose is to feed the biology already present or introduced with a suitable inoculant, rather than to supply a selected organism itself.
We need to know where the product will be applied and which inoculant, if any, is being considered. The soil conditions and mineral requirements remain part of the recommendation. A food blend and an inoculant have different purposes, and their preparation and compatibility must be confirmed before combining them.
