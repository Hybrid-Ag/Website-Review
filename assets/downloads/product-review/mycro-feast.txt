Overview
Mycro-Feast combines food sources for soil fungi and bacteria, including fish, kelp and humic and fulvic materials. Its purpose is to feed the biology already present or introduced with a suitable inoculant, rather than to supply a selected organism itself.
The intended root-zone placement, soil conditions and any inoculant being considered help the team review the application. Mineral nutrition still needs to be assessed alongside the microbial food source. A food blend and an inoculant do different jobs, so their preparation and compatibility need to be confirmed before they are combined.
