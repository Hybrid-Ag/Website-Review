REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Liquid Nitrogen and Iron for turf.
Nitro Iron supplies Nitrogen and Iron with Sulphur in a liquid turf fertiliser.
It is a combination to assess when the turf needs an Iron application alongside additional Nitrogen.

Overview
Nitro Iron combines a Nitrogen feed with Iron and Sulphur in a liquid turf formulation. It is assessed when active turf growth needs a Nitrogen contribution and the recommendation also calls for Iron, rather than as an Iron-only correction.
The material describes foliar and watered-in use. The turf area, existing feed and test information help the team review the combined contribution before choosing the method. Where additional Nitrogen is not required, compare this grade with a more targeted correction instead of treating poor colour alone as a reason to use it.
