Overview
Opti-Trace Boron Organic supplies Boron in a liquid formulation with amino acid chelates, fulvic extracts and saponins. It offers a targeted option where the crop needs this Element during tissue development or flowering.
A targeted product is different from a mixed trace blend. The agronomist can match its contribution to the Boron requirement without adding other trace Elements that the crop may not need.
