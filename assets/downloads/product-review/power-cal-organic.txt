Overview
Power-Cal Organic supplies Calcium with Boron in a liquid formulation containing natural chelators and carbohydrate compounds. It is considered as new roots, shoots and fruit tissue develop and the crop needs Calcium.
The recommendation should account for the crop’s Calcium status, irrigation and root conditions. The team can compare this product’s contribution with the need before choosing a targeted application or a custom liquid prescription.
