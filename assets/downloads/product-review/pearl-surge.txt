Overview
Pearl Surge combines Nitrogen and Sulphur with Humalite Carbon in a granular fertiliser. Its sowing and top-dressing uses give the agronomist options when the crop needs these Nutrients together.
Nitrogen and Sulphur are involved in plant proteins. The crop requirement and soil supply determine whether the fixed combination is appropriate, while the seeder or spreader influences placement and the application recommendation.
