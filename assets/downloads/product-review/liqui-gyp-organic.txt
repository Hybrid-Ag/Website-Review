Overview
Liqui-Gyp Organic supplies Calcium and Sulphur as a micronised gypsum suspension. It is a liquid option to assess for the root zone where these Nutrients are required or excess Sodium is part of the soil-management discussion.
Compare both Nutrient contributions with the soil results and the other nutrition being supplied. Where Sodium is involved, the recommendation also needs to consider soil structure and water movement. Use the Organic formulation’s own preparation and application directions, with current certification checked for the operation.
