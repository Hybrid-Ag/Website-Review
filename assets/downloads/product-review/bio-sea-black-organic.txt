Overview
Bio-Sea Black Organic provides a liquid kelp extract to consider alongside the crop’s mineral nutrition. Kelp contains naturally occurring compounds involved in root and shoot development. The product material includes active growth as well as post-harvest use in trees and vines while the plant remains active.
The reason for choosing it should relate to the crop and the planned application, not a fixed flowering schedule. Keep its own formulation and directions with the recommendation. The Organic product name does not replace the need to check the current certification and restrictions for the operation.
