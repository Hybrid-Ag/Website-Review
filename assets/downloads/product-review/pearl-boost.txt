Granular Carbon and Nutrient input.
Pearl Boost supplies Carbon with major and trace Nutrients in a granular formulation.
The staged material describes broadcast use in Broadacre and pasture and banded applications in tree crops.

Overview
Pearl Boost combines Carbon with major and trace Nutrients in a low-solubility granule. It provides a soil-applied option for Broadacre crops, pasture and tree crops where that contribution matches the soil recommendation.
Its granular format serves a different purpose from a quick foliar correction. The crop requirement, soil moisture and placement help the agronomist assess the soil application alongside any custom dry or granular prescription.
