Overview
Bio-Sea FKF combines fish hydrolysate, Ascophyllum nodosum kelp and fulvic acid in one liquid. The fish contributes amino acids and marine oils, while kelp contains naturally occurring compounds involved in plant growth. Consider the whole formulation alongside the crop’s mineral nutrition.
FKF is considered in Horticulture and Broadacre crops, with foliar and root-zone applications reviewed separately. The crop’s growth, soil conditions and nutrition already supplied help the team assess its use. Choose the timing and method for that requirement, using the current product directions rather than a fixed flowering or fruit-development schedule.
