Overview
Opti-Trace Iron Organic supplies Iron in a liquid formulation with amino acid, fulvic and saponin carriers. It is a targeted option for a crop with an identified Iron shortage.
The cause of pale growth needs to be established before an application is chosen. Plant results, soil conditions and root health help the agronomist assess the Iron requirement alongside the wider nutrition recommendation.
