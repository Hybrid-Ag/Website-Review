Overview
Opti-Trace Zinc Organic supplies Zinc with Sulphur in a liquid product. Zinc has a role in enzyme activity, so its supply is considered as part of the crop’s growth and trace Nutrient assessment.
A targeted correction needs to match the shortage identified in the crop. The agronomist can compare plant results with the Zinc already applied before recommending this product or adjusting the liquid prescription.
