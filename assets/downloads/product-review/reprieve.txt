Overview
Reprieve combines kelp extracts, soluble sugars and fulvic acid with mineral Nutrients. It is used around hot, dry conditions and has foliar and watered-in application options.
Heat and moisture stress need to be assessed with irrigation, root condition and the crop’s Nutrient supply. The team can consider whether Reprieve is useful in those conditions without treating an application as a substitute for water management.
