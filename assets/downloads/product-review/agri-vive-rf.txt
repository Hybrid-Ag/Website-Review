Agri-Vive RF | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Liquid nutrition for active growth.
Agri-Vive RF combines Nitrogen, Calcium, Potassium and Magnesium, with trace Nutrients, in one concentrated liquid.
Talk to us about your crop and soil requirements. We can help you work out whether RF is suitable or a prescription blend is needed.

Overview
New leaves, shoots and developing crops need a supply of Nutrients. The RF brochure draft describes use during active growth across vegetables, orchards and Broadacre crops, rather than a single crop or stage.
Before recommending RF, we look at how the crop is growing and what nutrition is already available. Your soil and plant test results help us work out what is needed, alongside the growing conditions and the equipment you use.

Application
A foliar application and a fertigation application are not interchangeable. Crop type, Nutrient supply, growth rate and growing conditions affect the rate and timing.
