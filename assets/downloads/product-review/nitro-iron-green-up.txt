An Iron-focused liquid turf feed.
Nitro Iron Green Up supplies Iron with Nitrogen and Sulphur in a liquid fertiliser for turf.
Its staged analysis is an option to compare where Iron is the main requirement while the application also contributes Nitrogen.

Overview
Nitro Iron Green Up is an Iron-focused liquid turf feed that also supplies Nitrogen and Sulphur. Its balance differs from Nitro Iron, so the two products should be compared by their own analysis rather than treated as interchangeable because of their names.
Review the Iron requirement with the turf’s existing feed, test information and growing conditions. The Nitrogen and Sulphur added in the same application still belong in the calculation. Foliar and watered-in methods are described for active turf growth, with the chosen method and amount confirmed for the area being treated.
