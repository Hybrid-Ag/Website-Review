Calcium and trace nutrition for fruit crops.
Fruitify supplies Calcium with Manganese, Iron, Zinc, Boron and Copper in a liquid formulation.
It is described for developing fruit where the crop requires the Nutrients carried in the product.

Overview
Fruitify combines Calcium with Manganese, Iron, Zinc, Boron and Copper in a liquid feed. Sugar-alcohol and other complexing compounds are part of the formulation described for developing fruit where those Nutrients are required.
The fixed combination needs to match more than a Calcium requirement alone. Compare each Nutrient contribution with the crop’s tests, existing applications and fruit load. That helps the team decide whether Fruitify provides an appropriate combination or a prescription should address different shortages and proportions.
