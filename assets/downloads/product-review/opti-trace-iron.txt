REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Chelated liquid Iron nutrition.
Opti-Trace Iron supplies Iron in a liquid formulation with several chelating components.
It is considered where soil and plant information identifies an Iron requirement during crop growth.

Overview
Iron is involved in enzyme activity and normal leaf function. Opti-Trace Iron supplies it in a chelated liquid, with foliar and watered-in options for a crop that needs an Iron correction.
Pale growth can have more than one cause. Crop testing, soil conditions and root health help establish whether Iron is short and whether the correction should be targeted or included in a custom prescription.
