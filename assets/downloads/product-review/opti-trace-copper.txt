Opti-Trace Copper supplies Copper with Sulphur in a chelated liquid formulation.
It is considered for a documented Copper requirement, taking account of test results and other Copper already supplied.

Overview
Copper is involved in enzyme activity within the plant. Opti-Trace Copper supplies it with Sulphur in a chelated liquid for a crop that needs a targeted Copper correction.
The formulation includes heptonate, sugar alcohol, amino acid and fulvic components with saponins. These named ingredients describe the product; crop testing and the wider Nutrient recommendation establish whether its Copper contribution is needed.
