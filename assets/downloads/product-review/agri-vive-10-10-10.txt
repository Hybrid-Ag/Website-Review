Combined liquid NPK nutrition.
Agri-Vive 10.10.10 combines Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium in similar proportions in a liquid fertiliser.
It is an option to assess where the crop needs these Nutrients together, rather than a larger addition of one Element.

Overview
Agri-Vive 10.10.10 supplies Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium in similar proportions. It gives the team a fixed-analysis liquid to consider when the crop needs those Nutrients together, rather than a feed concentrated on one Element. Each contribution needs to be counted against the crop’s requirement.
The same grade will not suit every soil or stage of growth. Soil and plant results help show whether these proportions are appropriate after earlier applications have been taken into account. Where the requirements differ, a prescription can supply a different balance rather than applying more of a Nutrient the crop already has.
