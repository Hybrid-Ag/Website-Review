Overview
Opti-Trace Bio-Check supplies Magnesium, Sulphur and Molybdenum with other trace Elements. It is a mixed Nutrient product to consider when crop testing identifies more than one requirement during growth.
When assessing Nitrogen nutrition, the team also considers the Nutrients involved in its use. Molybdenum contributes to the plant’s Nitrogen-processing enzymes and Sulphur is used in proteins. Bio-Check also supplies Magnesium, a component of chlorophyll. The crop results help determine whether this combination is needed or a more targeted prescription would be appropriate.
