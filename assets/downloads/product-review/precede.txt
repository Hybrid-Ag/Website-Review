Liquid trace nutrition for seed treatment.
Precede is described as a liquid seed-coating and priming product containing Zinc and Manganese with other components.
The staged ingredient description includes kelp, fish, fulvic acid, aloe vera and sugars for use before sowing.

Overview
Precede is a liquid seed-coating and priming treatment combining Zinc and Manganese-led trace nutrition with kelp, fish, fulvic acid, aloe vera and sugars. It places the formulation on the seed before sowing, rather than using it as an in-crop foliar feed.
The choice needs to account for the crop, seed and sowing conditions. The team can assess Precede alongside the establishment prescription and explain what further soil or crop nutrition may be needed as the crop develops.
