Overview
Opti-Trace Moly Organic supplies Molybdenum in a liquid formulation with amino acid, fulvic and saponin carriers. It is considered where the crop needs this trace Element alongside its Nitrogen nutrition.
Molybdenum is involved in nitrate metabolism and biological Nitrogen fixation. The crop’s test information helps establish whether its supply needs attention rather than assuming poor growth calls for more Nitrogen.
