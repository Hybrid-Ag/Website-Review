Combined liquid Nitrogen and Sulphur.
Nutri-Core SA supplies Nitrogen and Sulphur in a liquid fertiliser.
It is considered in the soil nutrition recommendation where the growing crop needs both Elements.

Overview
Nitrogen and Sulphur are involved in the formation of plant proteins. Nutri-Core SA supplies them together in a liquid soil fertiliser for a growing crop that needs both Nutrients.
We use the crop and soil information to establish whether both Nutrients are required. Previous applications also need to be included before we decide whether SA supplies the right balance or a prescription blend should be adjusted.
