Overview
BudMax combines Manganese, Zinc, Boron, Molybdenum and Iron in a liquid trace blend. The product can be considered where the crop needs several of these Elements, rather than assuming one trace Nutrient accounts for the whole requirement.
Manganese is involved in photosynthesis, while Molybdenum has a role in the crop’s use of Nitrogen. Review the trace requirement during crop growth or after harvest with the team. Plant results and the other Nutrients already supplied help determine whether BudMax’s proportions suit the recommendation.
