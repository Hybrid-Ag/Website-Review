Liquid NPK for a combined requirement.
Agri-Vive 18.6.14 combines Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium in one liquid fertiliser.
The staged grade is an option where Nitrogen and Potassium are required together, with Phosphorus also supplied in the application.

Overview
Agri-Vive 18.6.14 supplies Nitrogen and Potassium together, with Phosphorus in the same liquid application. It offers a fixed grade to assess when the crop’s requirements include that combination. The amounts of all three Nutrients need to be reviewed, even if one of them prompted the discussion.
A combined feed can be useful where its proportions match the crop. Soil and plant results, previous inputs and the nutrition still required help establish that match. If the crop needs a different balance, the recommendation can be tailored rather than increasing every Nutrient in the grade to meet the shortage of one.
