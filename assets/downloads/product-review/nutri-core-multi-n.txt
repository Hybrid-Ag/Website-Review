Liquid Nitrogen with Calcium.
Nutri-Core Multi-N combines Nitrogen with Calcium in a liquid fertiliser.
Its post-harvest use is aimed at the period when the crop still has an active canopy and needs those Nutrients before the season ends.

Overview
Picking the crop does not necessarily end the opportunity to supply nutrition. Nutri-Core Multi-N combines two Nitrogen sources with Calcium in a liquid fertiliser. It is used after harvest while leaves remain active, where the block needs those Nutrients.
The harvest result and Nutrients already applied help determine what is still needed. Leaf condition and the time remaining before the canopy declines also matter; a post-harvest application needs to be appropriate for that block, not repeated because it was used last season.
