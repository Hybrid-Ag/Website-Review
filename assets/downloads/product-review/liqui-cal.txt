REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

Micronised liquid Calcium suspension.
Liqui-Cal supplies Calcium in a micronised liquid suspension.
The staged material describes application through fertigation or watering into the root zone where the crop and soil require Calcium.

Overview
Calcium aids in the formation and strength of cell walls and is necessary for the development of plants. It is also required for early root growth and strong new top growth.
A suspension is not interchangeable with a soluble liquid. We need to consider the irrigation system and preparation equipment, as well as the Calcium requirement shown by the soil results. Allowing for nutrition already supplied helps us work out the amount required and whether this method is suitable for the crop.
