Overview
Liqui-Cal supplies Calcium in a micronised liquid suspension. Its finely divided mineral form provides a way to deliver Calcium through fertigation or by watering it into the root zone during the growing season, where soil and plant information identifies a requirement.
A suspension needs to be considered with the irrigation system and preparation equipment, not treated as interchangeable with a soluble liquid. Review the Calcium contribution against the soil results and the nutrition already supplied. The team can then assess the amount required and whether this delivery method is appropriate for the crop.
