Overview
N-Hance combines Zinc, Manganese, Copper, Boron, Iron and Molybdenum in a liquid trace blend for Broadacre crops. It is considered alongside the Nitrogen recommendation where the crop also requires the supplied trace Nutrients.
The material includes early leaf growth and later reproductive stages in cereals, canola and legumes. These are crop contexts, not a reason to add every trace Element at each stage. Soil and plant results help the team compare the fixed proportions with the actual requirement and decide whether another combination would better address the shortages.
