Combined nutrition after harvest.
Agri-Vive Post Harvest Boost supplies major and trace Nutrients in a liquid formulation for the period after picking.
The staged material describes use while leaves remain active, when the block’s nutrition is being reviewed before dormancy.

Overview
Picking is also a useful time to review the block’s nutrition before the next season. Post Harvest Boost combines major and trace Nutrients with plant and marine extracts for use after harvest while leaves and roots remain active. It is a combined feed to assess against the requirement, not simply a Nitrogen application because the fruit has come off.
The harvest result, earlier inputs and the condition of the canopy help the team decide what should follow. The product supplies Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium and Calcium alongside its trace content. Whether that fixed combination is appropriate depends on the block and the opportunity for the plant to take up the planned nutrition.

Application
Review the post-harvest opportunity while leaves and roots are still active. Foliar and fertigation applications have different requirements, so confirm the current method and timing for the block rather than applying from the harvest date alone.
Confirm the current product label, crop-specific recommendation, mixing and handling directions with the team.
