A liquid Magnesium and trace formulation.
Protex B Organic supplies Magnesium with a range of trace Nutrients in a liquid blend.
Its staged composition includes Manganese, Zinc, Molybdenum, Boron, Copper, Nickel, Cobalt and Iron; assess each contribution against the crop’s requirement.

Overview
Protex B Organic combines Magnesium with Manganese, Zinc, Molybdenum and other trace Nutrients in a liquid formulation. It is considered where the crop needs several of the supplied Elements together.
The fixed combination should be compared with plant results and previous inputs. That assessment helps the team decide whether this blend meets the requirement or whether a more targeted prescription is needed.
