The staged material describes use during crop growth, including developing tissue and fruit where the supplied Nutrients are required.

Overview
Opti-Cal supplies Calcium with Boron and Nitrogen in a liquid fertiliser. It gives the agronomist an option when the crop needs Calcium during growth, including while new tissue and fruit are developing.
Boron and Nitrogen must be included in the recommendation as well as Calcium. We use plant results and the crop stage to help establish the requirement. Irrigation and root condition also matter, along with the nutrition already supplied.
