Overview
Opti-Trace Copper Organic supplies Copper with Sulphur in a liquid formulation carried by amino acids, fulvic extracts and saponins. It is considered when the crop has an identified Copper requirement.
Copper is involved in enzyme activity within the plant. Test information and previous applications help the team establish whether a correction is needed and how much Copper the wider nutrition recommendation should supply.
