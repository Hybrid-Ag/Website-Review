Overview
Protex B combines Magnesium with Manganese, Zinc, Molybdenum and other trace Nutrients. It provides a liquid mixed-trace option for Broadacre crops and pasture when those Elements are needed during growth.
Magnesium is part of chlorophyll and Molybdenum is involved in Nitrogen use. Plant testing helps establish whether the supplied Nutrients are short; weather and grazing conditions also need to be considered when assessing pasture growth.
