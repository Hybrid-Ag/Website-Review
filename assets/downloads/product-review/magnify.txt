Overview
Magnify combines anionic and non-ionic surfactants with a Carbon source in a liquid adjuvant. The material describes its use in foliar nutrition mixtures where foaming and droplet behaviour need attention during preparation and spraying.
Those are application considerations, separate from the crop’s mineral requirement. Review the products in the proposed mixture and the spraying equipment with the team before deciding whether Magnify is needed. Its current dose and mixing directions must suit that use; it should not be recommended automatically for every tank mix.
