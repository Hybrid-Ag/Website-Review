Chelated liquid Zinc nutrition.
Opti-Trace Zinc supplies Zinc with Sulphur in a chelated liquid formulation.
It offers a targeted Zinc option where the crop’s requirement is not being met by soil supply and other nutrition.

Overview
Zinc is involved in enzyme activity and normal plant growth. Opti-Trace Zinc supplies it with Sulphur in a chelated liquid for a crop that needs a targeted Zinc correction.
The formulation includes heptonate, sugar alcohol, amino acid and fulvic components with saponins. The choice still begins with the crop’s requirement: test results and previous applications help establish whether a Zinc product or a wider prescription is needed.
