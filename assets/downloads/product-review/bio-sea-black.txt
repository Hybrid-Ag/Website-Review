Overview
Bio-Sea Black provides kelp extract in a liquid form. Kelp contains naturally occurring compounds involved in plant growth. The product is considered during active root and shoot development, alongside the mineral Nutrients required by the crop.
In perennial crops, the application discussion can also include the period after harvest while leaves and roots remain active. Review the condition of the crop and the nutrition already supplied with the team. This helps establish the reason for using a kelp product and any Nutrient shortages that need separate attention.
