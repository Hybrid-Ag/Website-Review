Micronised Calcium and Phosphorus suspension.
Liqui-Phos supplies Calcium and Phosphorus in a liquid suspension of finely ground guano.
The staged material describes root-zone and fertigation use where both Nutrients belong in the crop recommendation.

Overview
Liqui-Phos combines Calcium and Phosphorus in a suspension of finely ground guano, with naturally occurring trace Nutrients. The liquid format provides a way to deliver both main Nutrients through fertigation in the same application.
Selecting it for Phosphorus also means accounting for the Calcium supplied. Compare the complete analysis with the soil and crop requirement and the nutrition already planned. The irrigation system and preparation equipment help determine whether a suspension is appropriate for the proposed root-zone application.
