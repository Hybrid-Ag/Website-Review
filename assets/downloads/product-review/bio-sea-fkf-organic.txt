Liquid fish, kelp and fulvic nutrition.
Bio-Sea FKF Organic combines fish, kelp and fulvic components in a liquid formulation.
Its Nutrient contribution and the proposed application need to be assessed for the crop. The Organic formulation has its own directions and certification requirements.

Overview
Bio-Sea FKF Organic combines fish, kelp and fulvic components with a Nitrogen contribution in one liquid. Review its complete formulation with the team alongside the crop’s mineral requirements and the other nutrition being supplied.
The Organic formulation has its own analysis and application directions. Compare those with the crop’s requirement and the nutrition already being supplied, then confirm the method for the proposed treatment. Current certification and any restrictions need to be checked independently; conventional FKF documents cannot establish the directions for this product.
