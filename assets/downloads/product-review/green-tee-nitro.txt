Green-Tee Nitro | Hybrid-Ag working product reference
REVIEW DRAFT. Not a label, Safety Data Sheet or application recommendation.

A Nitrogen-led liquid turf feed.
Green-Tee Nitro combines Nitrogen with Iron, Magnesium, Manganese and Sulphur in a liquid turf fertiliser.
It can be assessed where active turf growth requires Nitrogen alongside the other Nutrients carried in the product.

Overview
Nitrogen is a component of chlorophyll which therefore makes it essential for photosynthesis.
Green-Tee Nitro is a combined feed, not a Nitrogen-only correction. We review the turf’s growth and existing applications alongside its soil and plant results. The other Nutrients must also be needed; a Nitrogen shortage alone does not justify supplying the whole combination.
