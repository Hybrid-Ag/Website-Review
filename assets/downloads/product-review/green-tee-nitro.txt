A Nitrogen-led liquid turf feed.
Green-Tee Nitro combines Nitrogen with Iron, Magnesium, Manganese and Sulphur in a liquid turf fertiliser.
It can be assessed where active turf growth requires Nitrogen alongside the other Nutrients carried in the product.

Overview
Green-Tee Nitro supplies Nitrogen with Iron, Magnesium, Manganese and Sulphur in a liquid turf feed. Nitrogen is part of proteins and chlorophyll, so its supply matters during active growth. The other declared Nutrients also need to be included in the nutrition calculation.
This is a combined feed rather than a Nitrogen-only correction. Review the turf’s growth and existing applications with its soil and plant results before choosing the grade. The aim is to match the amounts required, not to add every component simply because the canopy needs more Nitrogen.
