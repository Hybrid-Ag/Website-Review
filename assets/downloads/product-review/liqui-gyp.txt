Overview
Liqui-Gyp supplies Calcium and Sulphur together in a micronised gypsum suspension. Its fine particles provide a liquid option for fertigation, including where excess Sodium forms part of the root-zone problem.
Soil structure and water movement matter when assessing an excess-Sodium issue. The soil results help the agronomist review those conditions alongside the Calcium and Sulphur requirement, rather than recommending a gypsum product from the Calcium content alone. The suspension, delivery system and wider soil-management plan need to be considered together.
