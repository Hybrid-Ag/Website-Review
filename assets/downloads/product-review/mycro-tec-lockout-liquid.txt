Overview
The documented Lockout program pairs its liquid and powder components for preparation and placement around plant roots. It is considered within the biological part of a horticultural soil recommendation.
Confirm the actual liquid and powder being supplied, then obtain the instructions for that pair before preparation. They are different components, not interchangeable Nutrient grades.
