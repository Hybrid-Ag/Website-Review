From test results to a prescription blend

Soil and plant test results tell us more when we know what has happened in the paddock. We look at the rotation, previous applications and areas that grow differently, then work through what the next crop needs.

We use that information to recommend a Hybrid-Ag prescription blend that suits the paddock, the season and what is practical with your equipment and budget.
