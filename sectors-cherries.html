Horticulture/ Cherries

Cherry nutrition, stage by stage.

Cherry nutrition is considered across the season, from post-harvest and dormancy through flowering, set and fruit development. Hybrid-Ag uses the available tests and block history to guide prescription blends and recommendations at each stage.

Across the season

Use the harvest results to plan ahead.

Growth stage affects how a result is interpreted. Recommendations account for what is happening in the block rather than following a fixed calendar date.

01

Post-harvest

Review what the fruit and block showed, then consider the reserves and conditions carrying into next season.

02

Senescence and dormancy

Review the season’s results, soil conditions and tree health as leaves senesce and the trees enter dormancy.

03

Bud and flower

Early growth draws on the tree’s stored reserves as flowering begins.

04

Set and cell division

Review crop load and early fruit development during set and cell division.

05

Expansion and ripening

Water, uptake, canopy and crop load are interpreted against development and the target market.

06

Harvest and review

Use pack-out, quality and condition data when planning for the next season.

The Hybrid-Ag approach

Position. Pattern. Plan.

These questions help the team relate test results to the block and its current growth stage.

01Position

Where is this block now?

Crop stage, variety, crop load, recent conditions and the soil, water, plant or fruit evidence available today.

02Pattern

How did it get here?

Previous test results, post-harvest condition, changes through the season and application history.

03Plan

What should happen next?

Agree on what needs attention now, what can wait and what needs checking.
