Andrew Woodford

Andrew Woodford is a nutritional agronomist working with horticultural growers across southern and central Queensland and northern New South Wales. His work focuses on soil health and crop nutrition.

About Andrew

Horticultural experience across Queensland and northern NSW.

Andrew brings decades of experience working with horticultural producers. He is particularly interested in the relationship between soil conditions and plant health, drawing on observations from the farms he works with.

His work includes the Granite Belt, Sunshine Coast and Wide Bay, alongside horticultural growing regions in northern New South Wales.

Areas of work

Horticultural crops and soil health.

Horticulture Soil health Southern & Central QLD Northern NSW

Horticultural nutrition advice across southern and central Queensland and northern NSW, informed by soil and crop data.

Beyond the field

Growing food at home.

A former beekeeper, Andrew grows food for his family and tends his own land. His interest in soil and plants continues outside his work with commercial growers.

Andrew regards caring for the soil as part of growing food. He wants the land to be in better condition for the crops that follow, an aim that applies to his own place as well as his work with horticultural growers.
