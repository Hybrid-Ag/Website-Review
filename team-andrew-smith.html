Horticulture team leader

Andrew Smith

Andrew Smith is a generational cherry grower at Smiths Fruit in Taminick and leads Hybrid-Ag’s horticulture team. His work combines orchard experience, field observations and test results to guide crop nutrition.

About Andrew

Orchard experience at Smiths Fruit.

Andrew grows cherries at Smiths Fruit in Taminick, Victoria, alongside his wife Janine.

His work with Hybrid-Ag follows the cherry season from dormancy and flowering through fruit development, harvest and the post-harvest period. He uses orchard observations and test results to discuss nutrition with fellow growers.

At Smiths Fruit, Andrew’s stated aim is “using the wonders of nature along with our genuine love of cherries, to produce the best fruit ever!” He also contributes to seasonal presentations and grower education.

Beyond the field

Life at Smiths Fruit.

Through the cherry season, Andrew and Janine work closely with the orchard and packhouse team at Smiths Fruit. Picking is only part of the job: the fruit is processed, packed and sent out each day. Their son Karl is the orchard manager.

In 2024, a five-kilogram box of cherries from Andrew’s orchard was auctioned at Sydney Markets, with the proceeds supporting Little Wings. The charity provides flights and ground transport to help seriously ill children and their families in regional communities reach specialist medical care.
