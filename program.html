Crop nutrition programs

Crop nutrition suited to your farm.

We work with you to develop a nutrition plan for your paddock or block, using your test results and crop history. Prescription blends are planned around what needs to be supplied and how it can be applied, with recommendations reviewed as the season progresses.

The crop you grow, its stage of development and soil conditions help us determine the nutrition needed to work towards your production target. We discuss what is practical on your farm before preparing the recommendation.

Foundation

Build from the soil requirement.

Representative soil testing establishes the starting position. Where testing identifies a soil requirement, Hybrid-Ag can calculate the total elemental requirement in kilograms per hectare and use that calculation to help develop a prescription for the nominated area.
